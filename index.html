GET STARTED IN 3 STEPS

Live in Under 60 Seconds

No drivers, no extra server apps. OBS Studio 28+ has everything built-in.

1

Open OBS Studio 28+

In OBS Studio, navigate to Tools -> 'WebSocket Server Settings'. Ensure the WebSocket server is enabled.

> Menu: Tools
> [x] Enable WebSocket Server
> Default Port: 4455
2

Show Connect Info

Click 'Show Connect Info' to display the built-in local pairing QR code on your computer screen.

> Button: [Show Connect Info]
> Local IP: 192.168.1.xxx
> Protocol: obsws://
3

Scan with CueDeck

Launch CueDeck on your phone, point your camera at the QR code. You're paired instantly and ready to broadcast!

> Camera QR Viewfinder Active
> Auto SHA-256 Auth
> CONNECTED (0ms)
